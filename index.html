02 — Story

Story

Tye is a passionate songwriter, determined to make it in the music industry. His life takes a sudden new direction when he's signed by a powerful manager with a hip-hop empire. At first it seems like the break he's been looking for, but success comes with a hidden cost: constant manipulation, cutthroat games, and a growing distance from his soulmate, Olivia.

Follow Tye on an emotional journey as he must rely on his good heart and original music to survive the industry's sabotage and guide him to the place where he truly belongs.
