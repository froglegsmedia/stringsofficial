Writer, director, producer, composer

Tyler Forrest

From Louisiana to California, Tyler Forrest is a dynamic, multi-hyphenate artist whose passion for storytelling has established him as a promising voice for the future of cinema. As a true auteur, Tyler is an industry rarity, possessing deep, hands-on experience in the leading production roles of producer, director, cinematographer, editor, and composer. He writes all the original songs in his films.

Tyler's commitment to his craft is evident in his growing body of work. Following the success of previous films like The Dirty South, Search and Destroy, and Herman, he currently has several highly anticipated projects nearing completion (Strings, Balistyx, Suicide Disco) with several more in development.

Beyond his work behind the camera, Tyler is a dedicated musician, leading the music group JamNation for seven years at the legendary Rainbow Bar and Grill, where he discovered the lead cast of Strings. This profound musical background informs his filmmaking and elevates the emotional core of his projects.

At the heart of everything Tyler creates is a deeply human motivation: to reach people who are quietly living through something real — heartbreak, struggle, searching for belonging — and remind them they are not alone. His stories are not escapism; they are mirrors. Tyler believes cinema's greatest power lies in its ability to make a stranger in a dark theater feel genuinely, profoundly seen.

Tyler Forrest is not just making movies; he's bringing a vital, fresh, and unique perspective to the industry — one rooted in empathy, authenticity, and an unshakable belief in the connective power of art.

Cast

William "Killamind" Mossett

William "Killamind" Mossett is a powerhouse emcee, actor, and entrepreneur whose creative journey spans from the gritty streets of Philadelphia to the bright lights of Los Angeles. Born in Northridge and raised in Philly before making his return to LA, Killamind brings a coast-to-coast perspective to everything he touches—whether it’s a script, a microphone, or a business venture.

His career ignited on the airwaves as a standout character on POWER 99 FM’s Sex and Slow Jams, a role that bridged the gap between personality and performer. Since then, he has cemented his place in the hip-hop scene with five mixtapes and his latest genre-bending album, "Audiovision." A natural on stage, he is a fixture of the LA nightlife, hosting and performing at legendary residencies including Booyaka Lounge, Rainbow Bar and Grill, and Universal Bar and Grill.

Killamind’s transition to the screen is marked by a deep, thoughtful approach to storytelling. After years of collaborating through JamNation, his long-time creative partner Tyler cast him in the breakout role of Kel in the film Strings. As a natural performer with a kind soul and a knack for narrative, Killamind’s presence on screen is as compelling as his delivery on a track.

Currently, he’s pushing boundaries with Tyler in their band, Damn We Dope. Whether he is recording in the studio or headlining a show, Killamind remains a dedicated student of the craft and a relentless force in the independent art scene.

Cast

Kasey Yale

Kasey Yale is a versatile singer, songwriter, and actor known for his authentic storytelling and "genre-less" approach to music. A California native who relocated to Los Angeles to immerse himself in the creative scene, Yale has built a reputation as a prolific collaborator and a powerhouse performer in both the hip-hop and alternative worlds.

A cornerstone of his journey in the LA scene has been his involvement with JamNation, a long-running live music show and community where he has performed and collaborated for years. It was through the JamNation network that he formed some of his most significant creative partnerships, ultimately leading to his breakout acting role in the film Strings.

Yale’s musical journey began in high school fronting punk and rock bands before transitioning into a solo career that seamlessly blends pop, hip-hop, and soul influences. Often recognized for his work with longtime producer DiFiNiTiV, his discography includes several mixtapes and the 2025 synth-heavy album Midnight Ave. In the film world, he is increasingly recognized for his natural performance style and deep narrative instincts.

Driven by a mission to provide relief through art, Yale is also a co-founder of The Kingdom Brands, a partner company through which he releases music and apparel focused on authenticity and faith. Whether he is on stage at a JamNation event or in the studio, Yale continues to champion a creative vision rooted in emotional honesty and the power of connection.
